Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Luxury Africa Technology Center Ltd (LATC), Kicukiro District, Kigali, Rwanda. Contact: info@latcltd.rw.

Visit counting

We count visits to understand how many people find LATC and which pages help them. We use two tools:

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts every visit (page, approximate country, device type and the site you came from) without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Google Analytics runs with cookies switched off by default and receives only anonymous signals. Only if you choose "OK" on the visit-counting notice does it use cookies, so we can see more detail such as which buttons were used and how long a visit lasted. It never tells us who you are. If you choose "No thanks", cookies stay off.

Your settings on this device

Your chosen language, light or dark theme and visit-counting answer are saved only in your own browser so the site remembers them. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

When you contact us

If you write to us by WhatsApp, email or LinkedIn, we receive the details you send (for example your name, phone number and business) and use them only to reply to you and, if you asked for early access, to tell you when LATC Flow Suite launches. We do not sell your information.

Your rights

You may ask us to see, correct or delete the information you sent us, in line with Rwanda's law on the protection of personal data and privacy. Write to info@latcltd.rw.

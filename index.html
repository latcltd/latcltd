Inside LATC Flow Suite

Inventory you can trust. A market people trust.

Track every item from delivery to sale, and sell to customers nearby through verified shop pages, with sales, credit and reports in one place, even when the internet is down.

01Sell fast, even offlineTap or scan with any phone camera. Every sale is saved instantly and synced when the connection returns.+
  • Works on any phone, tablet or computer, no special hardware.
  • Scan barcodes with the phone camera or a USB scanner.
  • Each sale shows one tick when saved on the device, two ticks when safe on the server.
  • Cash, mobile money, card, credit and split payments.
02Stock you can trustEvery movement recorded with who, when and why. Real buying costs and an alert when a supplier raises prices.+
  • Deliveries record the supplier and the buying price.
  • Average cost is calculated for you, so profit is real.
  • A warning when your margin drops, and a red flag before selling at a loss.
  • Transfers between branches, damages and corrections, each with a reason.
03Credit under controlA limit for every customer, approval from your phone, part payments with receipts, and a clear view of who owes what.+
  • A customer with no limit gets no credit, even offline.
  • Cashiers ask, the owner approves from the phone, no password shared.
  • Each customer: total owed, overdue amount and credit left.
  • Part payments get their own numbered receipt; WhatsApp reminders in one tap.
04Price changes, made visibleLFS remembers what goods cost on the day you sold them on credit and shows what price rises cost you since.+
  • Example: sugar cost RWF 500 when sold on credit and RWF 800 today.
  • LFS shows: "Restocking these items now costs RWF 3,000 more."
  • Green when prices fell, orange when they rose.
  • See the true cost of giving credit and set smarter terms.
05A trusted online marketVerified shops, real prices and live stock colours. Customers find shops and products near them and order for pickup or delivery.+
  • Your page fills itself from your products: no website to build.
  • Every shop selling online is verified once with its registration documents.
  • You choose which products and prices are public; quantities are never shown.
  • Stock shows as a colour: green available, amber few left, red out.
  • Share your page on WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram in one tap.
06Order from the tableCustomers scan a QR code, order and follow every step live. Their receipt appears on their own phone.+
  • Steps shown live: sent, received, preparing, ready, served, paid.
  • Call the waiter or ask for the bill from the phone.
  • Orders go to the right station: kitchen, bar or coffee.
  • Staff get a sound alert for every new order and request.
07Receipts that can't be fakedEvery receipt carries a "Check this receipt" QR code that anyone can scan to confirm it is genuine.+
  • Each receipt has its own number, in an unbroken sequence per business.
  • Scanning the QR opens a page confirming the receipt, its number and its items.
  • Cancelled receipts stay visible with who, when and why.
  • Customers' phone numbers are masked on printed receipts.
08Secure by designEncrypted connections, approved devices only, roles that hide costs from cashiers, and a record of every action.+
  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • A new device must be approved by the owner before it can sell.
  • Each role sees only what it needs; refunds, discounts and credit need approval.
  • Every important action is recorded; data handled under Rwanda's data protection law.
